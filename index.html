Process

A shortlist you can act on, not a stack of CVs

STEP 01

Calibrate

A structured briefing session produces a written scorecard: must-haves, trade-offs, compensation reality and the two profiles most likely to succeed.

STEP 02

Map

We build the full addressable market for the role in Australia, not a keyword search. You see the map, including who we deliberately excluded.

STEP 03

Shortlist

Three to five candidates, each with a written assessment against the scorecard, motivation, notice period and salary expectation stated up front.

STEP 04

Land

Interview coordination, structured feedback loops, offer strategy and counter-offer management through to the signed start date.

✓ You always speak to the person running your search. No handoff.

✓ No candidate is presented without a written assessment.

✓ We tell you when a role is not placeable at the salary on offer.

Questions

Before you brief us

Do you work on contract or permanent roles?

We take permanent mandates first. Contract only when it is a genuine AI delivery seat, not a body-shop placement.

Which sectors do you cover?

Banking, insurance and superannuation are the core of the practice. We take selected mandates in health, government, energy and retail where the AI role profile is comparable.

How is Ashrel different from a large agency?

You deal with the person running the search. No account manager handoff, no CV forwarding, and no candidate sent without a written assessment against your calibration brief.

What does the engagement cost?

A percentage of first-year fixed remuneration, agreed in writing before the search begins. Retained and exclusive terms carry different rates, which we will quote on the call.

Do you guarantee the hire?

Every placement carries a replacement period agreed in the terms. In practice, calibration up front is what prevents the problem.

Can you work confidentially?

Yes. A large share of our mandates are unannounced roles or replacements, and are handled without the market knowing the client.

Tell us what you need to hire

Thirty minutes is enough to tell you if the role is placeable. No obligation, no database spam.
